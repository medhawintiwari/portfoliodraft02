## Tone of Voice

### Characteristics
- **Confident, not arrogant**: "I build exceptional web experiences" vs. "I'm the best developer"
- **Personal, not corporate**: "Here's how I think about this" vs. "Our team delivers solutions"
- **Specific, not generic**: "I built a real-time expense tracker that handles 1000+ concurrent users" vs. "I build scalable applications"
- **Concise, not verbose**: Let the work speak. One strong sentence > three mediocre ones
- **Creative, not quirky**: Professional with personality — not trying too hard to be fun

### Words to Use
- Build, craft, design, create, shape, engineer
- Interactive, responsive, performant, accessible
- Intentional, thoughtful, detail-oriented
- Experience, interface, product

### Words to Avoid
- Passionate (overused)
- Synergy, leverage, innovative (corporate)
- Guru, ninja, wizard, rockstar (cringe)
- Full-stack (too broad — use "creative developer" instead)
